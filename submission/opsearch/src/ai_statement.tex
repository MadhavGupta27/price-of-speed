\subsection{Use of generative AI}\label{sec:ai}

In line with Springer Nature's policy on large language models, we document their use here. A generative AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) was used under the authors' direction for: (i) first- and second-pass title/abstract screening against the criteria in Section~\ref{sec:method}, with every decision, exclusion code and rationale logged in the supplementary screening log; (ii) writing and testing the data-processing, optimisation and analysis code, which is verified by an automated test suite against hand-solvable and brute-force instances; and (iii) drafting and editing the manuscript text. The authors defined the research questions and scope, reviewed the screening decisions, code and results, and take full responsibility for the content of the article. No AI tool is listed as an author.
